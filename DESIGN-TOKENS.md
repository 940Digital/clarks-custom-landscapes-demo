# Design Tokens — Clark's Custom Landscapes

Forked from Template Zero. Full re-run of the token brainstorm — distinct from all four other businesses in this batch.

## 1. Identity

Zachery Clark runs Clark's Custom Landscapes in Denton — 4.3–4.4★ across 11–14 reviews (sources vary slightly). Unlike every other business in this batch, the draw here is almost entirely personality: reviewers write in ALL CAPS with stacked exclamation points ("This man is a lawn GENIOUS!!!!! He got everything PERFECT!!!!!"), naming Zach specifically. A visitor should feel that enthusiasm immediately — this is the one business in the batch where the owner's reputation *is* the specialty, more than any single service line.

## 2. Palette concept

No obvious literal name-association trap here ("Clark's Custom Landscapes" isn't evocative the way "Jurassic" or "Opulence" are), so the palette is grounded directly in materials: rich soil/loam, dried-grass paper tones, a bold saturated lawn green, and a warm terracotta clay for hardscaping — the two things Zach actually builds (living landscape + hardscape) as co-equal accents.

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Loam** | `#241C16` | Base dark surface — rich soil, not a stand-in for black |
| **Fescue** | `#F5F0E4` | Base light surface — dried-grass paper tone |
| **Ink** | `#1D1710` | Body text on light surfaces |
| **Zoysia Green** | `#3F7D32` | Primary accent — bold, saturated, matches the enthusiasm in reviews |
| **Paver Clay** | `#C1613B` | Equal secondary accent — hardscaping material |
| **Stone** | `#9C9284` | Muted support — borders, secondary text |

## 3. Type pairing

- **Display:** Unbounded (700/800/900) — a bold, geometric, high-impact face with real character.
- **Body:** Karla — plain, quiet, humanist.

**Why this pairing over the rest of the batch:** every other business in this set uses a display face tied to craft/refinement/sturdiness. Clark's needed something genuinely loud — the brief's own instruction to use "wildly different type treatments" is the whole design brief here, since the actual identity (an owner reviewers describe in all-caps exclamation) calls for oversized, unapologetic type rather than a quiet, tasteful one.

## 4. Signature element: The Exclamation

Neither a fill-bar (Jurassic) nor a checklist (Master Bin) fit an identity this loud. **The Exclamation** is a single giant "!" fixed at the bottom-left of the viewport that grows larger and more opaque as you scroll — literally scaling up the enthusiasm reviewers already bring in their own words, rather than borrowing a wayfinding device from a quieter brand.

## 5. Structural choices

- **Layout/type style assigned:** oversized, asymmetric typography — the hero headline breaks its own grid (an indented second line), and the reviews section uses large editorial pull-quotes alternating left/right instead of a card grid.
- **Lead section:** "Meet Zach," directly under the hero — per the brief's rule to expand the About/personal section significantly when the owner is clearly the draw, it was promoted to lead position rather than pushed down the page, since personality is literally the strongest specialty here.
- **Reviews:** large, oversized, quoted almost verbatim (caps softened slightly for readability, exclamation-heavy tone kept) with real first-name-plus-initial attribution.

## 6. Images

Real stock (Unsplash) — a detailed custom stone patio and a moody hardscaped garden path, chosen specifically to visually back up "down to the last, smallest detail" rather than generic lawn-mowing stock.

## 7. Flag for Owen — please read before outreach

Two things, independent of the site build itself:

1. **A negative review exists** (via Birdeye, from a customer named Tammy Wilcox): reports being charged via debit card nearly a week prior with no lawn service completed and no communication from the business. This is a **billing/no-show complaint**, not explicitly a licensing one.
2. **Licensing:** the original brief for this batch flagged "a possible license issue" mentioned in a review. Independent research (Birdeye, BBB, Yelp attempt blocked by a 403) did **not** turn up an explicit licensing complaint — BBB shows no complaints on file and no licensing information either way. This doesn't rule out the concern the brief referenced (it may be in a Google review not surfaced by these aggregators), so **please verify Zachery Clark's licensing status directly before pitching this one** — the billing complaint above is confirmed real; the licensing concern is not independently confirmed but also not ruled out.
